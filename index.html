about

I work across the full stack, and most of what I have built went to a client rather than a grader.

That has meant owning the schema, the API, the interface and the deploy on the same project, then being responsible for those decisions once real users arrived rather than once the demo ended.

The problems I find most worth solving are the ones with hard constraints: payments that have to reconcile, sessions that must not leak, market data that is already stale the moment you fetch it.
